Stream Shield

Terms you list here are replaced on web pages while protection is on. They are encrypted with your PIN and stay in this browser only.

Set a PIN

Use 4 to 12 digits. A longer PIN is harder to guess. The PIN is not saved. Terms stay locked after Firefox quits until you enter it again.

Terms are hidden. Show asks for your PIN before they appear.

One term per line. Matches ignore case and are not whole words, so a short term can hide unrelated text. When terms overlap, the longer one wins. Blank lines and repeated terms are removed on save. A few hundred specific terms is plenty. Turning protection off does not put the original text back; reload the page.
